# DailyBudget privacy

DailyBudget keeps your budget, transactions, day totals, savings entries, settings, and learned merchant rules in a database on your device.

- Nothing is uploaded to DailyBudget or to a DailyBudget server. There is no DailyBudget server or account.
- Screenshot text recognition runs on the device. Selected screenshot cache files are deleted after review or cancellation.
- Daily reminders are scheduled locally by the phone.
- CSV exports are created temporarily on the device and sent only to the app or destination you choose in the system share sheet. The temporary export file is then deleted.
- DailyBudget has no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party crash-reporting SDK.
- If a release crashes, the developer may use the aggregated crash information supplied by Google Play Console. DailyBudget does not add a separate crash-reporting service.
- App lock uses Android’s device authentication screen. DailyBudget does not receive or store your fingerprint, face data, PIN, or pattern.

Deleting all data in Settings removes all user-entered DailyBudget data and cancels its scheduled notifications. Uninstalling the app also removes its local app data under the operating system’s normal behavior.
